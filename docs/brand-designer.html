01 · the mark

The two delivered lockups

Both files have identical geometry — a thin keyline floating around hand-drawn lettering — and differ only in which colour goes on which layer. They are copied into the repo byte-for-byte as delivered; nothing here is redrawn.

02 · space & size

Clear space & minimum size

Keep open space around the lockup at least half its own height on every side, measured from the outer edge of the keyline. The keyline is only ~3% of the lockup's height, so it is what fails first when the mark gets small — below the minimums here it drops under a pixel and shimmers or disappears.

Two colours come from the designer's files; white is the reversed keyline. The fourth is derived here for the grey wordmark and is not a designer-specified colour.

04 · lettering

Custom lettering

The wordmark is drawn artwork, not a typeface, and ships as outlined vector paths — nothing to install and nothing to load. Two details are part of the design and must survive any adaptation: the O's counter is a speech bubble, and the i carries a dot.

06 · usage

Do & don't

Do

  • Use the primary on light and mid-tone grounds, the reversed on dark ones.
  • Use the SVGs as delivered, at any size — they're vector.
  • Keep clear space of at least half the lockup's height on every side.
  • Switch to an ink or white wordmark where the keyline would get too fine.
  • Keep the speech-bubble O and the dotted i exactly as drawn.

Don't

  • Recolour the keyline and lettering independently, or swap the two colours.
  • Put the primary on yellow or a busy photo — the keyline disappears. Don't put the reversed on light.
  • Add strokes, shadows, glows or bevels — the keyline is the outline.
  • Rotate, skew or stretch it; the slant is already in the lettering.
  • Retype or redraw the wordmark in a font.
  • Use the grey wordmark as the primary mark.
  • Go below 160px wide (lockup) or 96px wide (wordmark only).

For the designer to confirm

  • Sign-offThe three wordmark files (ink, white, grey) were derived from the delivered lettering layer, not drawn by the designer. They need a look before being treated as official.
  • MissingA square icon mark. The lockup is ~4:1, so app icons, favicons and avatars have nothing suitable to use yet — a monogram, such as the lead C, would fill that gap.
