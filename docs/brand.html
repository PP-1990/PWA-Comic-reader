02 · space & size

Clear space & minimum size

Keep open space around the mark at least equal to the height of the badge itself — don't let text, rules, or other logos cross into that margin. Below the minimum widths here, the outline and the tighter letter joins (the C, the A apex) start to clog up on screen.

Clear space≥ 1× badge height, all sides
Min width — badge72px
Min width — wordmark only120px
Favicon / app iconuse the flat variant
03 · color

Color

Three colors, always this combination on the primary mark — the gold and the ink are load-bearing; swapping either breaks recognition.

Comicaa Gold #FFD54F The badge fill. Always the ground the wordmark sits on.
Comicaa Ink #1A1408 The letterforms. A warm near-black, not pure black — at large sizes on a light ground it reads as a soft charcoal grey. That's intentional: don't "correct" it to #000.
Outline #000000 The badge's border only — pure black, distinct from the ink above.

05 · variants & files

Variants

Five files cover every placement. Don't recreate the wordmark by typing "COMICAA" in Bangers directly — the spacing here was tuned per letter-pair and won't match a live-rendered string.

comicaa-logo-badge.svg Default. Use this unless another variant is specifically called for.
comicaa-logo-flat.svg No rotation. For square frames — app icons, favicons.
comicaa-wordmark-ink.svg No pill. For stamping onto a light or colored surface.
comicaa-wordmark-white.svg No pill, reversed. For dark backgrounds only.
comicaa-wordmark-grey.svg No pill, muted (#9A9484 — this doc's own secondary-text grey). For low-emphasis placements: a footer credit, a watermark, anywhere the mark shouldn't compete for attention.
06 · usage

Do & don't

Do

  • Use the provided SVGs as-is, at any size — they're vector.
  • Keep the gold-and-ink pairing on the primary mark.
  • Keep the −1.4° tilt on the badge variant.
  • Give it clear space at least equal to its own height.
  • Use the white or ink wordmark-only files on backgrounds the pill doesn't suit.

Don't

  • Recolor the fill, outline, or letterforms.
  • Stretch, skew, or otherwise distort the proportions.
  • Add drop shadows, glows, or bevels.
  • Straighten the badge or rotate it further.
  • Retype "COMICAA" in Bangers instead of using the file — the kerning won't match.
  • Place it on a busy photo or low-contrast background.
  • Use the grey wordmark as the primary mark — it's for low-emphasis placements only.
