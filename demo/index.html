The idea

You already have the panels.

Now the reader can find them.

Reading order

Why readers care

A page built for print isn't built for a palm

Small screens, full pages

Shrink a print-lettered page to a phone and the dialogue goes illegible before the art does.

Guided, not zoomed

Guided View pans and zooms to every panel automatically, in reading order — no pinching, no hunting for the next line.

Runs on their device

No upload, no server round-trip. The reader's copy of the issue never leaves their phone.

Step 1 of 3

Drop in the archive

1

Open the .cbz or .cbr you already have — nothing to re-export first.

  • An on-device model reads the page and outlines every panel
  • This page: 6 panels found and numbered in under two seconds
  • No file ever leaves the browser

Step 2 of 3

Correct the draft

2

Every panel is a shape, not just a box.

  • Drag a corner to resize, an edge to trace an angled border
  • Add, delete, or redraw a panel from scratch
  • Drag panels in the list to fix reading order

Step 3 of 3

The reader's view

3

Export, and the reader gets an automatic path through the page.

  • One panel at a time, in the order you set
  • Pan & zoom, fade-to-black, or Cinematic (shown here)
  • Cinematic spotlights the current panel, dims the rest
